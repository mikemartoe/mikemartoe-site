The problem worth solving

Most agents don't need more motivation. They need their next sentence.

The phone gets heavy when you don't know how to open, what to ask, or how to respond when someone says, “We're just looking.”

Resource for Closers is built on a different idea: remove the guesswork from conversations, give agents a framework they can adapt to the person in front of them, and make the next follow-up easy to execute.

This is sales education, not a promise of appointments, clients, commissions or transaction outcomes.

From the field, not a content factory

“Don't give me a hundred clever things to say. Give me the right question to ask this person, right now.”

Resource for Closers · Guiding principle

One simple working method

Prepare. Converse. Follow through.

01

Prepare

Use the verified lead details to choose an opening, likely objection, and one honest next step.

02

Converse

Ask one thoughtful question at a time. Sound like a person, not a robot reading a script.

03

Follow through

Log what happened, respect the prospect's preferences, and create a clear follow-up task.

Good questions

Before you get started.

Do I need ChatGPT or another AI account?

You can read and adapt the sample scripts without AI. The complete AI prompts are designed for use in a compatible AI assistant you select; third-party terms and any charges for that service are separate.

Are the automations running inside TRDCRM now?

No. Volume 01 includes implementation blueprints, not installed or running CRM integrations. The TRDCRM-enabled edition is a future product goal.

Can I use these scripts in any state?

The writing is intended to be adaptable, but agents must check applicable laws, Do Not Call and consent rules, Fair Housing requirements, MLS rules, agency requirements and brokerage policies in their own jurisdiction before outreach.

Are these scripts copied from the book Exactly What to Say?

No. The product contains original Resource for Closers writing based on broad principles of clear, respectful sales communication. It is not affiliated with that book, its authors or its publisher.

Will these prompts guarantee more closings?

No. They are training and productivity resources. Conversations, appointments, clients and income depend on many factors, including the agent's skill, market conditions and the suitability of each opportunity.

Can my brokerage license the library?

Team and brokerage licensing can be discussed directly with Mike. No prepriced team purchase or hosted training platform is active at this time.
