About this home

Tucked away on a quiet dead-end street in a private garden community with lush greenery and manicured grounds. This move-in-ready 3BR/2.5BA Palm Beach Gardens townhome offers 1,580 SF and a long list of 2026 updates: interior and exterior paint, new light fixtures and fans, carpet, blinds, three toilets, refrigerator, and washer/dryer. The remodeled kitchen features white cabinets, black granite, mosaic backsplash, and stainless appliances, with a new stove, dishwasher, and A/C in 2023. Tile living areas, half bath with laundry, and a remodeled guest shower. Upstairs, the bedrooms open to a private balcony with a new 2025 fence, and the rescreened porch overlooks the gardens. Vacant and easy to show.
