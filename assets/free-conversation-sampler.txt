RESOURCE FOR CLOSERS — FREE CONVERSATION SAMPLER
By Mike Martoe | TRDCRM Conversation Confidence Series

Three original conversation starters. Not excerpts from any published book.
Use only accurate facts about your prospect and comply with all applicable
contact restrictions, fair housing rules, brokerage policy, and consent requirements.

1. Inbound Web Lead: The First 60 Seconds

OPENING
Hi [FIRST NAME], this is [AGENT] with [BROKERAGE]. You recently asked about [VERIFIED PROPERTY OR REQUEST]. Did I catch you with a quick minute, or would another time be easier?

IF THERE'S AN OBJECTION
“I was just looking.” → Absolutely. Most searches start there. What originally caught your eye: the property itself, its location, or the price?

NEXT STEP
Would it be useful if I checked the details on that property and sent you a short, accurate update, or would you prefer to leave it there for now?

-----------------------------------------------------------------

2. Dormant Lead: Restart Without Awkwardness

OPENING
Hi [FIRST NAME], it’s [AGENT]. We last connected around [VERIFIED TOPIC]. I’m checking whether that is still something you’re considering. Is now an inconvenient time?

IF THERE'S AN OBJECTION
“We put everything on hold.” → Understood. Would you like me to close out the search for now, or keep a note to check in only when you ask?

NEXT STEP
Would you prefer I leave the door open for you to reach out, or is there a specific month when a brief check-in would actually help?

-----------------------------------------------------------------

3. Pricing Conversations: Tell the Truth With Care

OPENING
I want to respect the number you hoped to achieve and also show you what the market evidence is telling us. Could we compare those side by side?

IF THERE'S AN OBJECTION
“Let’s just wait for our price.” → We can discuss that option. What matters most to you right now: reaching that exact number, selling within a certain timeline, or balancing both?

NEXT STEP
Would you rather set a date to review new evidence, or discuss a price adjustment based on the comparable properties we can verify today?
